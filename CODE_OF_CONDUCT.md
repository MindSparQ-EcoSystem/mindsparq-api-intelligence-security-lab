# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Focus feedback on ideas and evidence, not individuals.
- Accept differing experience levels and backgrounds.
- Protect privacy and do not publish credentials or personal information.
- Report harassment or unsafe conduct privately to project maintainers.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets, and encouragement of unauthorized access.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
